✨ AirNova is a compact, eco-friendly, and innovative air purification system designed by a team from General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University.
Its mission goes beyond technology – it’s about delivering cleaner, healthier air and uplifting the lives of underprivileged families exposed to harmful cooking smoke.


💡 Our Mission - To reduce respiratory health risks, minimize exposure to cooking smoke, and create equitable access to clean air for everyone.


🎯 Impact - AirNova was built with the vision of combining innovation, sustainability, and social good and It provides an affordable, space-conscious solution to improve air quality in underprivileged households.
